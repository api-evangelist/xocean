# XOCEAN

> XOCEAN is an Irish ocean-data company founded in 2017, headquartered at the Rathcor Technical
> Centre in Co. Louth, Ireland, with offices in the UK, US, Canada, Norway and Australia and more
> than 240 staff. It collects turnkey marine data with a fleet of low-carbon Uncrewed Surface
> Vessels (USVs) piloted over satellite from onshore operations centres, carrying multibeam
> echosounder (MBES), sub-bottom profiler (SBP), side-scan sonar (SSS), magnetometer and USBL
> payloads. Customers are offshore wind developers, subsea cable operators, national hydrographic
> offices, oil and gas operators and carbon capture and storage projects.

## Status

XOCEAN publishes **no public API**, no developer portal, no documentation site, no SDK and no
machine-readable contract. As of 2026-09-04, probes for `/llms.txt`, `/openapi.json`,
`/swagger.json`, `/api-docs`, `/.well-known/security.txt`, `/.well-known/agent-card.json`,
`/.well-known/agent.json`, `/.well-known/openid-configuration`,
`/.well-known/oauth-authorization-server`, `/.well-known/api-catalog` and
`/.well-known/ai-plugin.json` all returned 404 on `xocean.com` and `www.xocean.com`. The hosts
`api.xocean.com`, `docs.xocean.com` and `developer.xocean.com` do not resolve in DNS, and there is
no `xocean` GitHub organization. The company sells ocean data as project deliverables under
contract, not as a programmable product. This file is generated by API Evangelist from the catalog
profile; it is not published by XOCEAN.

One machine-readable surface does exist and is deliberately not catalogued as an API:
`https://xocean.com/wp-json/` answers 200 with the stock WordPress REST API root (407 routes
across 23 CMS-plugin namespaces). It is the marketing site's content-management layer, not a
product XOCEAN documents or supports.

## What XOCEAN measures

- Multibeam echosounder (MBES) bathymetry and backscatter
- Sub-bottom profiler (SBP) sediment penetration
- Side-scan sonar (SSS) seafloor imaging
- Magnetometer geophysical survey
- USBL positioning
- 2D ultra-high-resolution seismic (UHRS)
- Shallow geotechnical survey and environmental monitoring
